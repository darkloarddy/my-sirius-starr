You are so much more than you probably give yourself credit for. Your little habits, your personality, your humour, your thoughts, your interests, the way you express yourself, even the tiny things you might not notice about yourself — I adore all of it. I don't need you to be perfect, and I don't need you to be anything other than yourself. You don't have to constantly be happy, entertaining, pretty, or impressive to deserve love. Even on your quiet days, your difficult days, and the days when you don't feel like your best self, you are still just as precious to me.

And OMLLLL where do I even begin with your beauty?

Sometimes I genuinely wonder how someone can look so effortlessly beautiful. I wish you could borrow my eyes for just a moment, because maybe then you would understand why I find you so Beautiful. Maybe then you would see why I could look at you a thousand times and still think, there she is, my favourite person in the entire universe.

I want you to know that you matter. Your feelings matter. Your dreams matter. Your happiness matters. Your voice matters. I hope you never lose the little things that make you you. And whenever life gets overwhelming, I hope you remember that you are not defined by your worst days or your insecurities. You are a whole universe of wonderful things, and you deserve to see that in yourself.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for the memories, the conversations, the laughter, the random moments, and all the little things that have found a place in my heart.
